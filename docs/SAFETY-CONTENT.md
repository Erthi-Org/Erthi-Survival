# Safety content & review policy

**Do not publish survival advice as authoritative until reviewed.**

For each item, record: `id`, `scenario`, `language`, `jurisdiction`, `source_url`, `source_title`, `source_date`, `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`, `next_review_at`, `version`, `status` and `last_checked_at`.

Statuses: `draft`, `source-verified`, `expert-reviewed`, `published`, `withdrawn`. No unsupported jump from draft to published.

Content must:
- Align with official radiation emergency and public-health guidance and prioritize local authority instructions.
- Avoid universal timing/radius claims where geography and hazard matter.
- Clearly differentiate nuclear detonation, radiological release, contamination and radiation exposure.
- Explain the limitations of self-decontamination, first aid, KI and sheltering.
- Show publication/review dates offline and warn when guidance is stale.
- Be accessible in simple Arabic, with screened English translations.
- Never automatically infer that a device's position, shelter or message recipient is safe.

Product disclaimers are not a substitute for good instructions; surface the most urgent protective actions immediately.
